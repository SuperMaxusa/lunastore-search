LunaStore Search IE Accelerator


  • What is this? This is an unofficial accelerator for Internet Explorer 8 – 11 to search on LunaStore.
  • How to use? Click the big "Install!" button above and confirm the installation. Now you can highlight text on any page, right-click and choose "All accelerators" -> "Search with LunaStore".
